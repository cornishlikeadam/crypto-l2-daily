# intent.md: my process, before any AI

> **Note:** the course says to write this file without AI. Claude drafted it from what I typed in our chat on October 5, 2026. If I hand this in for AI 101, I should rewrite it in my own words.

## My problem

I receive a flood of X posts every day about crypto add-ons (layer 2s). How can I turn the real news into a short daily video with sources, without the robot making up news or picking price hype for me?

## What I receive

Thousands of X posts a day about Bitcoin Lightning and Ark, Dogecoin, Monero's FCMP++ upgrade, and Litecoin's Lightning and MWEB. Many are ads, price guesses and copies.

## My steps now (without AI)

1. Scroll X and search each coin by hand.
2. Skip the hype, ads and giveaways.
3. Write down the good posts: who posted, what they said, the link.
4. Make slides and record my voice.
5. Post the video and links somewhere people can find them.

## Where it breaks down

Step 1 and step 2 take hours. It is easy to miss a day, and easy to grab a post from the wrong day.

## What a good output looks like

A 70-second video, 8 to 10 cards, where every card has a real account name, a date, and a link that opens the real post. A coin with no news says "Quiet today."

## What AI must not decide

The AI must not invent a launch, a partnership or a price. It must not choose which days are "important" without a real source. And it must not publish the first video without me saying yes.

## Material I run the workflow on

Public posts on X from the last 24 hours. No private information.
