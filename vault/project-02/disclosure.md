# 🪪 disclosure.md

> [!abstract] ✍️ In one sentence
> **Claude wrote the code, the designs and the drafts of these notes; I chose the idea, the rules, the sources that matter, and every keep-or-reject decision, and I approved the first video.**

## 🤖 What the AI did

- 💻 Wrote **all the code**: the post collector, the filters, the video cards, the website builder, the course pages and the daily schedule.
- 🔎 Looked up **X's prices** and the **twikit bug**. Checked the 3 archive dates against **real sources**.
- 🛡️ Checked the **fixed twikit code** before I installed it.
- 🐞 Found **its own mistakes** in testing (wrong-day posts, fancy fonts, copies) and fixed them.
- 📝 Wrote the files in this folder from our chat. This includes a **draft** of [[intent]].
- 🎨 Made the **Black Platinum** design for the site and the video (October 6).

## 🧑‍⚖️ What **I** decided

1. 💡 **The idea:** which coins, 6 PM every day, 70 seconds, no price talk, name the accounts.
2. 🆓 Use the **free scraper** with **my own X account**.
3. ✅ **Approve** the first video. Then let it run by itself.
4. ➕ Ask for **more than a quote** on each slide, an **archive** and this **vault**.
5. 🎨 Ask for the **redesign**: black platinum, holographic, gold and green, easy to read for an 8-year-old.
6. 🔒 I typed **my own login codes** into the helper script. The model **never** saw them in a file.
