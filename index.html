Put it on your phone

The widget is a small card on your Home Screen. It shows the Bitcoin price, the fee, and today's top news.

  1. Install the free Scriptable app. Open it one time.
  2. Tap Download script. The file goes to the Files app.
  3. In Files, move scriptable.js into iCloud Drive › Scriptable.
  4. Hold your Home Screen. Tap +. Choose Scriptable. Tap Add.
  5. Hold the new widget. Tap Edit Widget. Choose the script.

Step 2 of 4

What it does

Every day at 6 PM, a robot reads X. X is a social media app.

The robot finds real posts about 4 coins: Bitcoin, Dogecoin, Monero and Litecoin.

Then it makes a 70-second video and a set of cards. Every card shows who posted and when.

If no sampled posts pass the filters, the card says "Quiet today." Other news may exist.

Step 3 of 4

The core

This is the automation. It runs by itself every day.

1⏰ At 6 PM, a timer on the Mac starts the robot.
2🔎 The robot collects posts from the last 24 hours.
3🧹 It removes ads, price guesses and copies.
4🎬 It makes the video and adds a voice.
5🌐 It puts the video online. Then it checks that the page loads.
